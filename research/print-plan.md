# First Field Edition Print Plan

## Deadline

- User departs for PLC on August 7, 2026.
- Target books-in-hand date: August 5, 2026.
- August 6 is reserved for emergency correction or local reprinting.
- Use local pickup or guaranteed expedited delivery; do not rely on economy shipping.

## Format

- Finished trim size: 5.5 × 8.5 inches, portrait.
- Binding: black plastic coil on the left.
- Printing: duplex.
- Interior: black-and-white on 60–70 lb uncoated, preferably warm-white or natural, text stock.
- Printed cover: 80–100 lb matte or uncoated cover stock.
- Protection: clear 7–10 mil plastic front sheet over the printed cover.
- Back: black, navy, or forest-green poly back sheet.
- Optional: rounded outside corners if available.

## Layout allowances

- Binding-side margin: at least 0.625 inch.
- Outside margin: approximately 0.4 inch.
- Top and bottom margins: approximately 0.5 inch.
- Keep all live text, page numbers, and decorative rules clear of the coil-punch area.
- Coil binding does not require a page count divisible by four.
- Avoid full-bleed interior design so the file remains compatible with local rapid-turnaround printing.

## Deliverables

1. Print-ready 5.5 × 8.5 PDF with coil-safe margins and nonessential hyperlinks removed from the visible page.
2. Screen/archive PDF with normal margins and clickable source links.
3. One locally printed physical proof before authorizing the complete run whenever the schedule permits.

## Production sequence

- Complete the edited manuscript and final pagination.
- Generate both PDFs and run visual, font-embedding, page-size, margin, and link checks.
- Print one local proof and inspect type size, line breaks, gutter clearance, duplex alignment, cover order, and plastic-sheet placement.
- Approve the production run only after proof corrections are incorporated.
- Keep a same-day local coil-bound job prepared as the fallback.

## Field-use note

Plastic end sheets improve splash and abrasion resistance but do not waterproof the uncoated interior. Carrying the booklet in a reusable zip bag is recommended for sustained wet conditions.
