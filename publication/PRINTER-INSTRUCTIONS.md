# Printer Instructions — First Field Edition

Use `output/pdf/wilderness-reflections-print.pdf` as supplied. It is a 5.5 × 8.5-inch, 138-page production PDF with mirrored coil-safe margins.

## Required page sequence

- Page 1: printed front cover, on the right side of the spiral when the book is closed.
- Page 2: intentionally blank reverse of the front cover, on the left when the cover is opened.
- Page 3: editorial note and first interior content page, on the right.
- Page 4: historical introduction, on the left.
- Page 5: table of contents, on the right.
- Page 6: `Life` section divider, on the left.
- Page 7: `Building the Bridge`, the first quotation page, on the right.

Do not insert an additional blank after the cover. Page 2 in the supplied PDF already provides the blank inside front cover and establishes the correct page parity for every subsequent mirrored margin.

## Production settings

- Finished size: 5.5 × 8.5 inches, portrait.
- Print at actual size (100%); do not fit or scale to another page size.
- Duplex, black and white.
- Bind with a black plastic coil on the left.
- Interior: 60–70 lb uncoated stock.
- Printed cover: 80–100 lb matte or uncoated cover stock.
- Clear 7–10 mil plastic front sheet over page 1.
- Black, navy, or forest-green poly back sheet.
- Preserve the PDF page order and existing mirrored margins.

## Bindery checkpoints

When the book is open, the wider margin must always face the spiral:

- even-numbered pages are on the left and have the wider right margin;
- odd-numbered pages are on the right and have the wider left margin.

As a quick check, page 44 must be on the left of the spiral and page 45 on the right. The blank space on both pages must be greater along the spiral edge than along the outside edge.
