## [The Greatest Wonder]

*One-sentence remark recorded during Emerson's 1871 Yosemite journey.*

The greatest wonder is that we can see these trees and not wonder more.

*Ralph Waldo Emerson*

## [The Smaller We Come to Feel Ourselves]

*Two-sentence excerpt from “The Significance of Place.”*

The smaller we come to feel ourselves compared with the mountain, the nearer we come to participating in its greatness. I do not know why this is so.

*Arne Næss*

*Concise excerpt from Lessing's statement on truth.*

The search for truth is more precious than its possession.
