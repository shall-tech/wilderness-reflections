## [A Profound, a Fundamental Need for Areas of Wilderness]

*Two selected clauses from consecutive portions of "The Need for Wilderness Areas."*

We have a profound, a fundamental need for areas of wilderness. . . . This need is for areas of the earth within which we stand without our mechanisms that make us immediate masters over our environment.

*Howard Zahniser, principal author of the Wilderness Act*

## [There Is a Limit to the Number of Lands of Shoreline]

*Selected passage from Carhart's December 1919 memorandum.*

There is a limit to the number of lands of shoreline on the lakes; there is a limit to the number of lakes in existence; there is a limit to the mountainous areas of the world, and . . . there are portions of natural scenic beauty which are God-made and . . . which of a right should be the property of all people.

*Arthur Hawthorne Carhart*
